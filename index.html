Based in Columbus, Ohio, Jenny & The StreetWalkers bring progressive bluegrass and roots music to festival and venue stages. Razor-tight three-part vocals, twin fiddles and bold arrangements meet slinky grooves and crowd shoutbacks. From haunting melodies to full-throttle dance-floor energy, The StreetWalkers pull the whole room into the show.
